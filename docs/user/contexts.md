# Contexts

A **context** is a place you work in - Work, Home, a café with only the Supernote. It decides what Daily, Week, Month, Projects, Areas and the Weekly Review show, so the day in front of you holds only what fits where you are. A context like Home may show almost nothing: that is rest, not a gap.

## Choosing one

The chip in the tab bar, left of ✕, names the active context (**Everything** when none is chosen). Tap it: the menu lists your contexts with a one-line summary and how many next todos each shows, and **Everything**. Tap one to switch; the status line confirms it ("Context: Home"). **Edit contexts ›** opens Settings → Contexts.

The context stays until you choose another, also after closing Kept GTD. Each profile has its own contexts.

## What a context shows

A context has a **Shows** list and a **Hides** list. Each holds areas, projects and tags:

- an **area** brings its projects, and the loops and threads in them;
- a **project** is just that project;
- a **tag** brings every todo and meeting with that tag or a tag under it: `#retro` also brings `#retro/demand`. Loop and thread tags work the same way.

The rules:

- **Hides wins**: what Hides names is left out, even when Shows names it too.
- A context with a Shows list shows only what that list names. Projects and areas no context mentions show only under **Everything**.
- A context with an empty Shows list shows everything except what Hides names ("everything but work").
- The **Inbox** and todos **due today or earlier** always show, in every context.
- **Meetings** are never hidden. Meetings outside the context are drawn faint (dimmed), or shown as usual if you choose that for the context.

## Where it applies

- **Daily**: the next todos of the context; meetings outside it dimmed.
- **Week and Month**: meetings outside it dimmed.
- **Projects and Areas**: only the context's projects and areas.
- **Weekly Review**: each step lists the context's items and todos. The overview shows "+2 in other contexts" next to a step's count. **Reviewed** and the reminder dot count every context: reviewing a step under Home marks it reviewed for all.

Not filtered: the Inbox tab, a project's or area's own page (Current), Settings, Engage, and the thread and loop overviews.

## The hidden line

A filtered tab ends with a line like "Hidden by Work: 9 todos · 2 loops · 1 thread · Show". **Show** shows everything on that tab until you leave it; the context itself stays.

When Quick Add files a todo where the active context doesn't show it, the status line says so: "Saved to Atruvia · hidden by Home".

## Setting up contexts

In **Settings → Contexts**, tap **+ New context**, give it a name, then tap areas, projects and tags under **Shows**; switch to **Hides** for what to leave out. **Meetings outside this context**: Dimmed or Shown as usual. The preview counts how many next todos it shows. **Save** stores it at once; **Delete** removes it (your files are not touched).

When you rename a project's or area's folder and Kept GTD offers to rename its home notebook, its contexts follow the new name. Otherwise **Run Integrity Check** (Settings → Advanced) lists contexts that name a project or area that isn't there any more.
