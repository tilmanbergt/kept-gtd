# Week and Month

The Week and Month tabs are for planning. Both work the same way: meetings and focus blocks on the left, Quick Add and **Given time** on the right.

## Week

The left side shows the week as a grid of meeting chips, one box per day. Use ‹ and › to move between weeks. Tap a chip to show the meeting's card and its context panel on the right (see [Meetings](meetings.md)), with "‹ Back to Week 42 · Thu 15.10. · 2 meetings ›" above it; tap a day's header to open that day's meetings on the right.

The right side shows Quick Add and **Given time this week** (see below).

## Month

The left side lists every day of the month. It shows meetings you marked as **monthly highlights** - big things like a trip, an offsite or a birthday. Use ‹ and › to move between months, and tap a day to see all its meetings on the right.

Tap one of the day's meetings on the right, and its card takes Quick Add's place, as on every meeting list (see [Meetings](meetings.md)): the prep or review tick, **M** and, once the meeting is over, **Reflect**.

To make a meeting a monthly highlight, switch on the small **M** next to **+ Add** when adding it, or **M** on its card later. Highlighted meetings show the same M in 2-line rows. A short name in brackets at the end of the title, for example "Art of Transformation Session (AoT)", is used as the short form in the month list.

The right side has **Given time this month** (see below).

## Given time

Given time shows where your time went, from your schedule alone, in two lists:

- **Given time this week**: your areas - each with everything scheduled in it and in its projects - and the projects without an area, the one with the most time first. A project with more time than an area comes before it. The Inbox is never listed.
- **Loops and counterparts**: the loops and threads that got time, again the most first. The same meeting counts here once more. Only those with at least **1 h a week** (**2 h a month**) are listed, so the list stays short; when none reaches that, it says "Nothing above 1 h this week". Each context can set its own threshold, see [Contexts](contexts.md).

The time is on the right (`3.5 h`; an all-day event counts as `1 day`), the grey line below says how many focus blocks and meetings, how many todos were done and the next todo (`2 blocks · 1 meeting · 3 done · next: Draft chapter`). Tap a row to open its project or area, or the overview of its thread or loop.

**Worked on this week** lists the areas and projects with todos done but nothing scheduled. Cancelled meetings don't count.

Under it, **Tend: 3 threads · 2 loops quiet ›** counts the threads that need tending - nothing planned in the next 14 days and no next or I-owe todo - and the loops that need a look (see [Weekly Review](review.md)); tap it to open the Weekly Review's **Tending threads**. With a context active ([Contexts](contexts.md)), only what it shows counts, and the last line says how many owners it hides.

## Meeting length

Type the time into the meeting's time field in one of these forms:

- `15` or `9.30`: starts at that time
- `15-16.30`: start and end
- `1d`, `2d`, `3d`: all day, for one or more days

An empty time field means one whole day. Meetings over several days appear on every day they cover.
