# Week and Month

The Week and Month tabs are for planning. Both work the same way: meetings and [focus blocks](meetings.md#focus-blocks) on the left, [Quick Add](quick-add.md) and [Given time](week-and-month.md#given-time) on the right.

## Week

The left side shows the week as a grid of meeting chips, one box per day. Use ‹ and › to move between weeks. Tap a chip to show the meeting's [card](meetings.md#the-meeting-row-and-its-card) and its [context panel](meetings.md#the-context-panel) on the right, with "‹ Back to Week 42 · Thu 15.10. · 2 meetings ›" above it; tap a day's header to open that day's meetings on the right.

The right side shows [Quick Add](quick-add.md) and [Given time this week](week-and-month.md#given-time).

## Month

The left side lists every day of the month. It shows meetings you marked as *monthly highlights* - big things like a trip, an offsite or a birthday. Use ‹ and › to move between months, and tap a day to see all its meetings on the right.

Tap one of the day's meetings on the right, and its [card](meetings.md#the-meeting-row-and-its-card) takes Quick Add's place, as on every meeting list: the [prep or review](meetings.md#preparation-and-review) tick, **M** and, once the meeting is over, [Reflect](meetings.md#reflection-page).

To make a meeting a monthly highlight, switch on the small **M** next to **+ Add** when [adding it](quick-add.md#adding-a-meeting), or **M** on its card later. Highlighted meetings show the same M in 2-line rows. A short name in brackets at the end of the title, for example "Team offsite (Offsite)", is used as the short form in the month list.

The right side has [Given time this month](week-and-month.md#given-time).

## Given time

Given time shows where your time went, from your schedule alone, in two lists:

- **Given time this week**: your areas - each with everything scheduled in it and in its projects - and the projects without an area, the one with the most time first. A project with more time than an area comes before it. The Inbox is never listed.
- **Loops and counterparts**: the [loops](loops.md) and the [counterparts](threads.md) that got time, again the most first. The same meeting counts here once more. A counterpart sums all its threads: *jordan* counts `#sparring/jordan` and every other type used with jordan together (and its `#wf/jordan` todos), and its grey line names the series (`sparring`). Counterparts belong to an area (with its projects) or a project without an area; the same name in two areas shows twice, with the area: "jordan · Coaching". Loops stay one row each. Only those with at least **1 h a week** (**2 h a month**) are listed, so the list stays short; when none reaches that, it says "Nothing above 1 h this week". Each context can set its own threshold, see [Contexts](contexts.md).

Each row's kind - AREA, PROJECT, LOOP, COUNTERPART - is a small badge right of the name. The time is on the right (`3.5 h`; an all-day event counts as `1 day`), the grey line below says how many [focus blocks](meetings.md#focus-blocks) and meetings, how many todos were done and the next todo (`2 blocks · 1 meeting · 3 done · next: Draft chapter`). Tap a row to open its project or area, the [overview of its loop](loops.md#the-loop-overview), or a [counterpart's overview](threads.md#thread-or-all-of-the-counterpart) with all its threads.

**Worked on this week** lists the areas and projects with todos done but nothing scheduled. Cancelled meetings don't count.

Under it, **Tend: 3 counterparts · 2 loops quiet ›** counts the counterparts that [need tending](threads.md#tending-counterparts) - nothing planned in the next 14 days and no next or I-owe todo - and the loops that need a look; tap it to open the Weekly Review's [Tending threads](review.md#tending-threads). With a [context](contexts.md#what-a-context-shows) active, only what it shows counts, and the last line says how many owners it hides.

## Meeting length

Type the time into the meeting's time field in one of these forms:

- `15` or `9.30`: starts at that time
- `15-16.30`: start and end
- `1d`, `2d`, `3d`: all day, for one or more days

An empty time field means one whole day. Meetings over several days appear on every day they cover.
