# Kept GTD help

Kept GTD is a calm GTD and PARA workspace for the Supernote. Your projects, areas, todos and meetings live as plain text files in your own folders. The plugin shows them in a few focused views and helps you review them regularly.

## The tabs at a glance

- The [Projects](projects-and-areas.md) and [Areas](projects-and-areas.md) tabs: all your projects and areas, [create new ones](projects-and-areas.md#creating-one)
- The [Daily](daily.md) tab: today and tomorrow - your schedule and the todos that matter now
- The [Week](week-and-month.md#week) and [Month](week-and-month.md#month) tabs: plan the week and the month, and see where your time went ([Given time](week-and-month.md#given-time))
- The [Inbox](quick-add.md#the-inbox) tab: everything captured but not yet filed
- The [Current](projects-and-areas.md#the-current-tab) tab: the project or area you opened last, with its todos, meetings, files and notes
- The [Review](review.md) tab: the Weekly Review, one step at a time
- The [Settings](settings.md) tab: [folders](settings.md#folders), [contexts](contexts.md#setting-up-contexts), [Note setups](note-setups.md), [features](settings.md#features), [advanced options](settings.md#advanced), [About](settings.md#about)

The chip next to **?** shows the current [context](contexts.md) (Everything, Work, Home …); tap it to [switch](contexts.md#choosing-one).

**?** opens this help, starting at the page for the tab you are on. Bold underlined words are links to other pages or sections; **‹ Back** returns to where you were. Bold words without a line are buttons and labels as the app shows them, `this` is what you type or what stands in your files. **✕ Close** in the help closes it again; the tab behind it stays exactly as it was. ✕ closes the plugin. When you open Kept, it reads the files you changed outside it again. To read all files again, use [Reload all files](settings.md#advanced) in Settings → Advanced.

## The status line

The strip under the tab bar is the status line. Every message of Kept shows there: what was saved and where, what went wrong, and questions before Kept creates, renames or deletes something.

- A question has its buttons on the right, for example **Create** or **Use mine**. Nothing happens until you tap one; ✕ cancels.
- ✕ also clears a message once you have read it.
- A message too long for the line ends with …; tap it to read it in full.
- When several messages wait, ‹ and › with "1/3" page through them. The most important one comes first: questions before errors, errors before the rest.

## Opening Kept GTD from a note

When you open Kept from a note that belongs to a project or area, it opens that project on the [Current](projects-and-areas.md#the-current-tab) tab. From any other note it opens [Daily](daily.md). If you opened a note from inside Kept and come back from that same note, Kept returns to the screen you left.
